## Working paper
*Wetlands as nature-based cooling infrastructure for data centers in Northern Virginia* (Zou, 2026). Preliminary, not yet peer reviewed. The manuscript is available on request.

## Main results
- **Study 1, surface temperature.** Wetland minus urban daytime land surface temperature: winter −0.06, spring −2.88, summer −3.29, fall −1.67 °C (MODIS, 2019 to 2024).
- **Study 2, water use.** One data center's groundwater withdrawal rises by about 43,600 gallons per month for each +1 °C (p = 0.002), and fell by about 1.2 million gallons per month after its 2023 cooling retrofit (p < 0.001).
- **Robustness.** With month-of-year fixed effects the temperature coefficient is no longer significant (β = 0.030, p = 0.32). I report this openly; it is why the next phase uses many facilities and wet-bulb weather.
